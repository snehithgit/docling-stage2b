Technical evidence

Help the app understand your manual

Check useful instructions, tables and diagrams so the app can find better evidence when you ask a question.

Ask a question

What can I do here?

Find information such as troubleshooting steps, alarms, safety notes, settings and part numbers. For a diagram or flowchart, AI can read the visible labels and connections, then you check them against the original image.

What you get: checked diagram information that can support answers with a source-page citation. This page does not rewrite the manual or guarantee that every answer is correct.

  1. Choose your manual below. Its detected information loads automatically.
  2. Choose an item and read its original text or image.
  3. For a diagram: click “Read this diagram with AI”, then compare the labels and connections.
  4. Confirm only what you checked. Afterwards, test a question in Ask. Refresh the machine search index if Ask reports it is out of date.
Do I need to use this for every book?

No. Start here when an answer misses information in a diagram, flowchart or boxed note. Ordinary text already goes through the book pipeline. Use Review for OCR, table or page-layout corrections.
