How the pipeline works
Convert→Analyze→Verify→Finalize→Chunk→Embed→Search

Open any book to see its current stage and the single next action. Raw Docling files stay unchanged; corrections are applied as overlays. AI reviews propose changes; save or apply a human correction to change the effective book. Search availability does not certify complete extraction or validated evidence. The Stage blockers count is library-scoped; the global Needs attention banner also includes diagnostics and human decisions outside the library stage summary.
